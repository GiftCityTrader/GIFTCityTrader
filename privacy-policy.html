Privacy Policy

How Gift City Trader collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit giftcitytrader.com. Last updated: 3 September 2026.

This privacy policy explains what happens to information when you use giftcitytrader.com (the "Site"), operated by Gift City Trader ("we", "us", "our"). By using the Site you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the Site.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us voluntarily

We only receive this if you choose to send it:

  • Contact details — your name, email address or phone number, when you email us or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Enquiry content — whatever you write in that message.
  • Enrolment information — if you join a paid programme, the details needed to deliver it and to raise an invoice.

There is no signup wall, newsletter form or account system on this Site. You can read every article without giving us anything.

Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, our hosting infrastructure and our analytics and advertising partners automatically receive certain technical information when a page loads:

  • IP address (often truncated or anonymised by the processing service)
  • Browser type and version, operating system and device type
  • Pages viewed, time on page, and the page or search that referred you
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address, typically at city or country level
  • Date and time of the visit

This is aggregate technical data. We do not use it to try to identify you personally.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. We and our partners use them for:

  • Essential functions — remembering your cookie-consent choice so we do not ask again on every page. This is stored locally in your browser.
  • Analytics — understanding which articles are read and where readers come from, in aggregate.
  • Advertising — serving and measuring advertisements, and limiting how many times you see the same one.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can decline non-essential cookies using the notice shown on your first visit. Blocking cookies will not stop you reading anything on this Site.

3. Advertising, Google AdSense and third-party vendors

This Site displays advertising. Advertising revenue is what keeps the learning library free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other sites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Third-party advertisers we do not control may also place cookies. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Google's own practices are described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising is not editorial Advertisements are labelled and kept separate from our articles. Advertisers have no influence over what we publish, and we do not write content in exchange for advertising placement.

4. How we use information

  • To answer your questions and provide the services you asked for
  • To deliver a course you have enrolled in, and to raise invoices
  • To understand which content is useful and improve it
  • To display and measure advertising
  • To keep the Site secure and detect abuse
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent or trade contact lists.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who need it to operate the Site, and only for that purpose:

CategoryPurpose
Hosting and content delivery providersServing the website
Analytics providersAggregate traffic measurement
Advertising partners, including Google AdSenseServing and measuring ads
Payment processorsCollecting course fees, where applicable
Government or legal authoritiesOnly where required by law

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiry emails and messages: up to 24 months, then deleted unless you became a student.
  • Student and billing records: as long as required by Indian tax and accounting law.
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregate, per the retention settings of the analytics service.
  • Advertising cookies: per the lifetime set by each advertising vendor.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over information we hold about you:

  • Access — ask what we hold
  • Correction — have inaccurate information fixed
  • Deletion — ask us to erase it, where no legal obligation requires us to keep it
  • Objection and restriction — ask us to stop or limit certain processing
  • Withdrawal of consent — where processing is based on consent
  • Portability — receive a copy in a usable format
  • Complaint — raise the matter with your data protection authority

These rights reflect obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, for readers in those regions, the EU/UK GDPR and the CCPA. To exercise any of them, email contact@giftcitytrader.com. We will respond within 30 days, and may ask you to verify your identity first so that we do not disclose data to the wrong person.

8. Children

This Site is not directed at children under 18, and our courses are not offered to minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to enquiry and student records to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, so while we take reasonable measures we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Links to other sites

Articles sometimes link to exchanges, regulators, tools and other external resources. Once you follow such a link, this policy no longer applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party site, including sites reached through advertisements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site, our partners or the law change. The revision date at the top of the page will be updated, and material changes will be highlighted on the homepage for a reasonable period. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the revised policy.
