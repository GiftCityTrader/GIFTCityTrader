About Us

Who We Are and What We Actually Do

Gift City Trader is a stock market education brand run by a practising Indian trader. We teach how markets work. We do not sell tips, calls or guaranteed returns.

Why this exists

Gift City Trader started for a straightforward reason: the gap between what is freely available about trading and what a person actually needs in order to trade sensibly is enormous, and almost nobody is filling it honestly.

There is no shortage of content. There are thousands of hours of free videos, an endless supply of screenshots showing profitable trades, and a large industry selling signals to people who have never been taught to evaluate a signal. What is genuinely rare is a patient explanation of the mechanics — why a level matters, what an indicator is actually computing, why an option loses value when the underlying moves in your favour, and how much of your capital any single idea deserves.

That gap is what this project is built around. Everything we publish and everything we teach is aimed at making a learner independent — able to form a view, size it properly, and know in advance where they were wrong.

Who runs it

Gift City Trader is run by an active Indian market participant with over six years of experience trading equities and equity derivatives, alongside teaching. The teaching is not a departure from the trading; the examples used in every session come from charts and positions in the same markets students are learning to navigate.

We think this matters. A great deal of trading education in India is taught by people who have never held a position through a genuinely bad week. Understanding what a drawdown does to decision-making is not something that can be learned from a textbook, and it is the part of the curriculum students consistently say changed the most for them.

How we teach

Four principles shape everything on this site and in the course:

1. The logic comes before the tool

Anyone can be told to buy when RSI crosses 30. Far fewer are told what RSI computes, when that reading is misleading, and which market conditions invalidate it entirely. We teach the second version, because the first stops working the moment conditions change and leaves the student with nothing.

2. Limitations get equal time

Every indicator, every pattern and every strategy has conditions under which it fails. Those failure conditions are taught alongside the technique, not omitted to make the material look more impressive. A student who knows when a tool does not apply is safer than one who believes it always does.

3. Risk before returns

Position sizing, stop placement and drawdown management are not an appendix at the end of the syllabus. They run through every module, because the difference between traders who last and traders who do not is almost never the quality of their entries.

4. Independence is the goal

The measure of whether the teaching worked is whether the student stops needing the teacher. We are not building a subscription that a person is expected to renew indefinitely. We are trying to hand over a skill.

What we do not do

Being clear about this matters more than any marketing claim:

  • We are not SEBI-registered as an investment adviser or research analyst. We provide education. We do not provide personalised investment advice, and we cannot legally do so without that registration.
  • We do not give buy or sell recommendations — not on this website, not in articles, not in the course, and not privately.
  • We do not promise returns. Anyone promising you a fixed monthly return from the market is either misinformed or misleading you. Trading outcomes are uncertain by nature.
  • We do not manage anyone's money or accept funds for trading on a client's behalf.
  • We do not sell trading signals or run a paid calls service.

If any of this is disappointing, that is genuinely useful information — it means the thing you are looking for is not what we offer, and you have saved yourself time and money.

How we produce content

The articles in our learning library are written in-house by the Gift City Trader team, drawn from live trading experience and from questions students actually ask. They are reviewed and updated when market structure, regulation or exchange rules change — Indian derivatives rules in particular have changed several times in recent years, and outdated material is worse than no material.

Where a claim depends on regulation, tax law or exchange rules, we point readers to the primary source rather than asking them to take our word for it. Where something is genuinely uncertain or contested, we say so instead of manufacturing confidence.

Advertising and how this site is funded

This website carries third-party advertising, including advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertising revenue is what allows the learning library to stay free and open to everyone rather than sitting behind a login.

Advertisements are clearly marked and are separated from editorial content. Advertisers have no input into what we write, and no article is influenced by an advertising relationship. Our privacy policy explains what data is collected in connection with advertising and how you can control it.
