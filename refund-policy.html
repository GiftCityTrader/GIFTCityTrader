Refund & Cancellation Policy

What happens if you change your mind, or if we cannot deliver. Last updated: 3 September 2026.

This policy applies to paid programmes purchased directly from Gift City Trader. Free content on this website carries no charge and therefore no refund.

1. Before the batch starts

If you cancel more than 7 days before your batch start date, you receive a full refund of the fee paid, less any payment-gateway charge actually incurred.

If you cancel within 7 days of the start date, you may transfer your seat to a later batch at no cost, or request a refund of 75% of the fee. The retained portion covers seat allocation and preparation already committed.

2. After the batch starts

Once live sessions have begun and course material has been shared, the material cannot be returned, and refunds are therefore not available. This is standard for live cohort-based training and is stated clearly before payment.

If a genuine emergency prevents you from attending — medical or bereavement — contact us. We will normally offer a transfer to a future batch at no additional cost. We would rather you complete the course later than lose it entirely.

3. If we cancel or cannot deliver

If we cancel a batch, or are unable to deliver the programme you paid for, you receive a full refund or a free transfer to the next batch — your choice, no conditions.

An individual session postponed and later delivered is not a cancellation.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Email contact@giftcitytrader.com from the address used at enrolment, with your name, batch and payment reference.
  2. We acknowledge within 2 working days and confirm eligibility under this policy.
  3. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few additional days to credit it.

5. What is not refundable

  • Fees where the participant has attended sessions or received course material, except as covered in section 3.
  • Enrolments terminated for breach of the terms & conditions — for example sharing paid material or recording sessions without permission.
  • Payment-gateway or bank charges already incurred on the original transaction.
No outcome-based refunds We do not offer refunds based on trading results. We teach a skill; we do not control how, when or whether it is applied, and no honest education provider can guarantee a market outcome.

6. Disputes

If you are unhappy with how a refund request was handled, reply to the same email thread and ask for it to be reviewed. We would much rather resolve it directly than have you raise a chargeback. Any unresolved dispute is governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Gujarat.
