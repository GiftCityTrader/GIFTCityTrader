What we cannot help with

To save you time, these requests will politely be declined every time:

  • "Should I buy X?" We are not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, so we cannot give you a personalised recommendation on any security. That is not caution on our part — it is the law.
  • "Give me a target and stop-loss for my position." Same reason. We can explain how to derive one; we cannot hand you one.
  • "Will you trade my account?" We do not manage anyone's money, in any form.
  • "Can I get a guaranteed return?" Nobody can offer this honestly. If anyone does, that is your signal to walk away.
