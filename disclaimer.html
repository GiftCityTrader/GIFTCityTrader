3. Market risk

Trading and investing in securities markets involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every person. In particular:

  • You can lose your entire invested capital. This is a normal outcome, not an unlikely one.
  • Derivatives can cost you more than you invested. Futures positions and short option positions carry theoretically unlimited loss potential, and losses can exceed the margin deposited.
  • Leverage magnifies losses exactly as much as it magnifies gains.
  • Gaps cannot be stopped out. A stop-loss order does not protect against a price gap between one session's close and the next session's open.
  • Liquidity can disappear. In stressed markets you may be unable to exit at or near the price you expected.
  • Technology fails. Broker platforms, internet connections and exchange systems can and do go down at inconvenient moments.

SEBI's published studies of individual traders in the equity derivatives segment have repeatedly found that a large majority lose money over a financial year. We state this plainly because any education provider who does not is failing you.
